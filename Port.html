03 / How I Work

Principles I Ship By

My operating system for managing platform complexity, AI reliability, and cross-functional alignment.

01.

Deconstruct messy pipelines first

Before writing a PRD, I dig into support logs, usage telemetry, and analyst workflows to locate the exact system bottleneck rather than treating symptoms.

02.

Human-in-the-loop by default

Autonomous agents fail silently without guardrails. I design explicit evaluation criteria, fallback triggers, and user verification checkpoints where reliability matters.

03.

Balance velocity with modularity

Shipping fast shouldn't mortgage the platform. I partner closely with backend engineers to evaluate build-vs-buy trade-offs and minimize downstream technical debt.

04.

Translate across every room

Whether aligning founders on GTM priorities, backend engineers on API schemas, or civil administrators on regulatory compliance, clear specs keep execution predictable.
