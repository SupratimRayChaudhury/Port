03 / How I Work

Principles I Ship By

My operating framework for platform systems, AI reliability, and cross-functional governance.

01.

Deconstruct messy pipelines first

Before writing a PRD, I dig into customer support logs, operational feedback, and usage telemetry to uncover recurring system bottlenecks rather than shipping surface-level patches.

02.

Human-in-the-loop by default

Agentic workflows fail silently without explicit guardrails. I architect model output evaluation criteria, latency monitoring, fallback triggers, and human verification checkpoints.

03.

Balance velocity with modularity

Rapid feature deployment shouldn't create downstream technical debt. I partner with backend teams to evaluate build-vs-buy trade-offs and scale shared platform capabilities.

04.

Translate across every stakeholder room

From founders and GTM teams to systems engineers and non-technical civil administrators, I use crisp technical specs and KPI dashboards to keep roadmap execution predictable.
